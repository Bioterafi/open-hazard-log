# Workflow: reviewing results on the ward round (made-up demo document)

1. The nurse in charge opens the viewer on the ward tablet and selects the ward.
2. Results arriving since the last round are listed with the newest at the top.
3. Values outside the reference range are shown in bold. Reference ranges come from the adult range table.
4. The doctor reviews each abnormal result and taps Acknowledge once a plan is made.
5. Acknowledged results drop off the list. The list is shared by everyone logged in on that ward.

During busy periods results can arrive after the round has started; the list refreshes every five minutes.
