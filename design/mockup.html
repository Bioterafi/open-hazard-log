scan · prompt scan-v1 · claude-opus-5-5
Title
Paediatric reference ranges missing, so abnormal results look normal
Cause
Range table has adult ranges only; no age banding. [software]
Effect
Abnormal result not highlighted for a child.
Harm
Delayed treatment, e.g. missed hypoglycaemia or hyperkalaemia.
Controls
Design: age-banded ranges.Test: paediatric range cases.Process: hide ranges for under-16s until fixed.

Your rating

Rate first. The agent's suggestion appears after.

Why the agent proposed this

The intended-use statement covers "all inpatients", which includes children. The range lookup only holds adult values, so a child's result is compared with adult limits.

Assumptions

The viewer is used on paediatric wards. No separate paediatric system exists at the site.
